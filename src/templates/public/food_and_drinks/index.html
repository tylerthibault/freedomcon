Plan Your Weekend

Food & Drinks

The Gorge has a rotating mix of quick bites, savory meals, frozen treats, and beverage stands throughout the venue.

Vendor Lineup Notice

Food and drinks available for purchase at the event.

Vendors open Friday night when gates open and at 8:00 AM Saturday morning for breakfast. Feel free to bring your own food to the campground. Food vendors depend on projected attendance and may change as ticket sales grow closer to the event.

See FAQ page to learn more about bringing snacks into the venue.

Breakfast & Morning Favorites

Options include breakfast, coffee, sandwich, waffles, muffins, and fresh fruits.

Mosh Burger

Mosh Burger brings you patties that are built to thrash. Made from 100% U.S., hormone-free beef, stacked high with melty American cheese, rich caramelized onions, and a punchy burger sauce to finish. Rock your hunger away.

Rebel Hen Crispy Chicken

At Rebel Hen, every tender is fried with pride, seasoned to perfection, and packed with a whole lotta flavor. It’s real food with real flair—crispy, juicy, and made to steal the spotlight. Be sure to check out our house-made sauces, and you’ll see why we keep our fans cluckin’ for more!

Ziggy's Magic Pizza Shop

A NYC-inspired pizza joint conjured by Ziggy the Wizard, serving up delicious cheesy pies, made with a touch of magic. Come early and feed your vibe!

Koko's

Koko’s Bavarian celebrates the historical German culture of Central Texas with a menu featuring specialty sausages, hot dogs, Bavarian pretzels, and Belgian-style fries. Founded in Austin, Texas, Koko’s is named for co-founder Konstantin Prinz von Bayern (aka Koko), the Prince of Bavaria—yes, the actual Prince.

Jade Lane Dumplings

Jade Lane serves up juicy dumplings with a modern Asian twist. Pinched, fried, drizzled, tossed & gone—each bite is bold, craveable, and ready when you are. Eat dumplings. Be happy.

Tacos del Centro

At Tacos Del Centro, Mexican street eats come hot and fresh with bold flavor in every bite. From carne asada to pollo asado, go on—have a taco.

General Concessions

From classic concert bites to refreshing drinks, our general concessions offer something for everyone. Find your favorites at our conveniently located concession stands and get back to the show!

CVT Soft Serve

Premium Soft Serve Ice Cream in a Pouch…No, Seriously! No machine, no prep, no mess. Just thaw, squeeze and eat! Flavors include Chocolate and Vanilla, with a Twist on the way!

Subzero Frozen Drinks

Turn up the chill! Subzero is the ultimate slushie experience, blending bold, electrifying flavors that hit as hard as the bass. Cool off with summer flavors like strawberry or limeade, made with all-natural ingredients.

ColdRush Shaved Ice

ColdRush serves up shaved ice with bold, punchy flavors like Very Berry, Piña Colada, and Miami Vice that play just like your favorite song.

Riffresher

Crisp, vibrant, and exclusively at our venues, Riffresher is made for those who thrive in the front row and live for the encore. These drinks are made with real juice and come in bold energy, lemonade, and cream soda flavors. Stay refreshed, stay amped.

Liquid Death

Liquid Death will not kill you. But make no mistake, our infinitely recyclable cans of premium low-calorie beverages will absolutely murder your thirst. And it doesn’t stop there. Beverages include: Mountain Water, Soda-Flavored Sparkling Water, and Iced Teas.

Before You Go

  • Vendor availability can change by event date.
  • Menus and pricing are set by the venue and partners.
  • Mobile ordering and line speed vary by show.
  • Drink selections can vary by event and stand location.
